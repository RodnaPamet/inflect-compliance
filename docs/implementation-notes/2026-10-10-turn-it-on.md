## This step's subject no longer exists

Step 6d turned on the local decision model. That model was **Laya**, and Laya
was retired on 2026-10-14 after #3490 measured it answering from prompt
**length** rather than content: filler text carrying no identity information
changed 39 of 69 match decisions, every one of them onto the first candidate.

So this note documents work that was deliberately undone. The `laya` service,
its pinned image and its compose entries are gone;
`tenant-security-settings.ts` now refuses `legacyMatchAiMode=LOCAL_ONLY` with
that measurement as the reason, and the phases document's Laya criteria are
struck through rather than ticked.

That is worth a note rather than silence. A reader finding the retired
references in the history should be able to tell "this was removed because it
was measured to be wrong" from "this was never finished".

## What survives the retirement

- **Runtime images pinned by digest** (#3401) became a standing governance
  pillar independent of Laya, and still applies.
- **The measurement discipline.** #3446 set the deadline and concurrency from
  measured throughput rather than a guess, and #3414 corrected the vendor wire
  table against a real Laya after the design document's version proved wrong on
  three fields. Both are the habit that later measured Laya out of the system.
- **The mode's refusal path.** `LOCAL_ONLY` is refused at the settings seam
  with the reason attached, so an operator reading the error learns what
  happened rather than finding an empty dropdown.
