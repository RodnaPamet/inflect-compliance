## Mapping and level changes

### Item 5 — AIUC-1 ↔ SOC 2 (`aiuc-1-to-soc2.yaml`, `soc2-to-aiuc-1.yaml`, v2)

#3369 split SOC 2's CC6 into CC6.1–CC6.8, and the catalogue's access controls
moved with it: TSC-CC6-1 (provisioning and revocation) links CC6.2 and CC6.3,
TSC-CC6-3 (periodic access review) links CC6.3 and CC4.1, and CC6.1 keeps only
authentication/MFA (TSC-CC6-2) and encryption (TSC-CC6-4). The crosswalk still
read CC6.1 as "the CC6 series", so neither access control reached AIUC-1.

- **B007 → CC6.3 (was CC6.1), INTERSECT.** B007 is role/attribute-based access
  to the AI system, reviewed quarterly with each change justified. CC6.3 is the
  criterion that authorises and modifies access by role and reviews it
  periodically; CC6.1 (access-security architecture) does not speak to either.
  INTERSECT, not SUPERSET: meeting CC6.3's unspecified cadence does not meet a
  quarterly one. The B007 → CC6.1 edge is retired: MFA and encryption controls
  give no coverage of an access review.
- **A003 → CC6.3 added, INTERSECT.** A003 gives an agent an identity whose
  access is "granted, reviewed and withdrawn like anyone else's" and bounded by
  its role — least privilege and review, which is CC6.3. The A003 → CC6.1 edge
  stays: enforcing the bounds in retrieval and permission logic is access
  security architecture.
- Other CC6.1 edges (A005, A008, B006, B008, D003, E005) were re-read against
  CC6.1's own text and kept.
- Both headers and `coso-to-soc2.yaml`'s header now describe the split CC6
  representation (the COSO file maps nothing onto CC6–CC9).

### Item 6 — NIS2-AM → ISO 27001 (`nis2-to-iso27001.yaml`, v2)

Both edges carried a SUPERSET rationale under a SUBSET label. Re-judged from
the texts (NIS2-AM: MFA, secure communication, identity management, and its
checklist's "define and enforce privileged access management controls"):

- **NIS2-AM → A.8.2: SUPERSET.** Privileged access management is a named
  component of NIS2-AM, and A.8.2 is "restrict and manage the allocation and use
  of privileged access". The rationale was right; the label was wrong.
- **NIS2-AM → A.5.17: INTERSECT.** A.5.17 requires controlled allocation and
  management of authentication credentials, which NIS2-AM does not spell out;
  A.5.17 does not require MFA. They overlap on authentication. Here the
  rationale ("one aspect of NIS2") was the wrong part and is rewritten.

## Decisions

- **The toast copy is a required argument, not an English default.** A default
  would let the next caller ship English to a Bulgarian user with nothing
  failing. The copy is strings only (the versioned description is the raw
  message with `{version}` substituted), so it is equal across renders and can
  sit in a save callback's dependencies.
- **Assets and policies bump the tenant version directly** rather than gaining
  aggregation entities: no aggregation cache reads them, and the SSR payload is
  keyed on the tenant-wide version alone.
- **Existing databases are not reconciled.** `applyCatalogFile` upserts
  requirements and template links and never removes either, so the next
  catalogue apply adds V3.7.1, V7.1.1 and V7.1.2 and their links, while the
  `OWASP-ASVS` rows for V2.6.1 and V11.1.6 stay in place, still categorised L1
  and still linked to their templates. Removing them is a data change for the
  owner to decide, not part of this fix.
