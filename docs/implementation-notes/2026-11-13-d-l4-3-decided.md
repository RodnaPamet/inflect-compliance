## Design

The retriever benchmark (`.github/workflows/retriever-benchmark.yml`) ran
once, as run 43413063708 on `main` `32d3038`, over the seed set
`tests/fixtures/rag/retriever-seed-set.json`
(`sha256:297f425a247bb6f6800528187765f404fee8e9f879c41f2727897fe11573e92f`):
250 cases, 50 of them Bulgarian questions over the English corpus (BG → EN).
The workflow's summary job recomputed every figure from each record's raw
answers.

The summary's per-reranker-group rule chose `multilingual-e5-small` in every
group. D-L4-3's rule over all nine pipelines (the smallest within 0.05
recall@10 of the best, overall and on the Bulgarian slice):

- best: `multilingual-e5-small` + `bge-reranker-v2-m3`, 0.960 / 0.880;
- `multilingual-e5-small` + `bge-reranker-base`: 0.946 (0.014 behind) and
  0.840 (0.040 behind), within the margin;
- `multilingual-e5-small` alone: 0.899, 0.061 behind overall, outside it.

What the run did not capture: reranker latency (the summary has the columns;
the figures were not taken from it), and per-pipeline memory, which the
benchmark does not measure. The latencies above are the CI runner's, not the
VM's.

## Owner decisions, 2026-11-13

1. **D-L4-3 adopted:** embedder `intfloat/multilingual-e5-small`, reranker
   `BAAI/bge-reranker-base`. The lower first-place ranking (MRR@10 0.863
   against 0.936) is the price of not resizing. `bge-reranker-v2-m3` is the
   better-ranking alternative if the VM is ever resized for another reason;
   not a commitment.
2. **The 50 Bulgarian translations are accepted unreviewed.** Recorded in the
   roadmaps, not in the fixture: editing the fixture changes its hash.
3. **pgvector is absorbed into `postgres`'s existing 2,048 MiB limit.**
   Measured peaks 406 MiB (C0) and 332 MiB (2026-11-13); 406 + about 512 is
   about 918 MiB, and 1.5 times that is about 1,377 MiB, under the limit.
   Re-checked once vectors are loaded.

## The capacity arithmetic

| | MiB |
| --- | --- |
| Today's limits (C1) | 10,496 |
| Embedding (`multilingual-e5-small`, weights about 0.47 GB at fp32, plus TEI), about 1 GB | 1,024 |
| Reranking (`bge-reranker-base`, weights about 1.1 GB at fp32, plus TEI), about 1.5 GB | 1,536 |
| PII detection, 0.7 GB | 717 |
| Guard rails, 1 GB | 1,024 |
| Vectors (absorbed) and the parser (nightly window) | 0 |
| **Total** | **14,797** |

Against the 14.5 GB line (14,848 MiB): fits, about 50 MiB to spare. Against
the stricter reading, measured host memory minus 1.5 GB (16,003 − 1,536 =
14,467 MiB): about 330 MiB over. C1b gives the margin: the harness scanner
from 512 to 256 MiB (owner-approved), and `app` and `worker` re-sized from the
capacity recorder's history. Every addition is an estimate until its phase
measures it. Retrieval does not trigger D-CAP-1's resize.

## Decisions

- The candidate pins in `scripts/retriever-benchmark/candidates.ts` are left
  as they are: this is a docs-only change, and pinning the chosen revisions
  belongs with the service that deploys them (Retriever 1.1).
- The margin on the main reading is about 50 MiB, not a few hundred; the
  roadmap says so rather than rounding it into comfort.
