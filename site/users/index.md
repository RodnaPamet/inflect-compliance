Inflect Compliance helps your organisation run its compliance programme in one place: the risks you face, the controls that address them, the evidence that proves they work, and the audits that check it.

## Find your way around

The guides follow the app's own sidebar, from top to bottom. If you are looking at a page in the app, its guide sits under the same heading here.

- **{{ui:nav.dashboard}}**, at the top of the sidebar, is your workspace's home page. Start with the [{{ui:dashboard.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/overview/dashboard.md), and the [{{ui:onboarding.wizard.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/overview/onboarding.md) if your workspace is new.
- **{{ui:nav.govern}}** covers your assets, risks and controls: the [{{ui:assets.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/govern/assets.md), the [{{ui:risks.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/govern/risks.md) and the [{{ui:controls.list.pageTitle}}](../../docs/user/guide/govern/controls.md) page.
- **{{ui:nav.comply}}** covers the day-to-day work of staying compliant: [audits](../../docs/user/guide/comply/audits.md), [tasks](../../docs/user/guide/comply/tasks.md), the [{{ui:calendar.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/comply/calendar.md), [tests](../../docs/user/guide/comply/tests.md) and the [{{ui:evidence.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/comply/evidence.md).
- **{{ui:nav.manage}}** covers governance and reporting: [policies](../../docs/user/guide/manage/policies.md), the [{{ui:vendors.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/manage/vendors.md), the [{{ui:agents.register.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/manage/agents.md), [processes](../../docs/user/guide/manage/processes.md) and [reports](../../docs/user/guide/manage/reports.md). You see {{ui:nav.agents}} and {{ui:nav.reports}} only when your role can open them, and {{ui:nav.processes}} only when your workspace has the process canvas turned on.
- **{{ui:nav.admin}}**, the gear icon at the bottom of the sidebar, covers running the workspace: its [{{ui:admin.members.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/admin/members.md), [{{ui:admin.sso.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/admin/sso.md), [{{ui:admin.security.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/admin/security.md) and [{{ui:admin.apiKeys.pageTitle}}](../../docs/user/guide/admin/api-keys.md) pages. Owners and Admins see it.
- **Organization** pages are for an organization that groups several workspaces. Open one from the workspace switcher at the top of the page. Its sidebar has its own sections, {{ui:org.nav.portfolio}} and {{ui:org.nav.manage}}: start with the [{{ui:org.dashboard.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/organization/portfolio-overview.md), the [{{ui:org.tenants.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/organization/workspaces.md) list and the organization's [{{ui:org.members.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/organization/members.md).
- **{{ui:nav.account}}** pages are about you rather than a workspace. Click your picture or initials at the top right of any page for [{{ui:account.profile.profileTitle}}](../../docs/user/guide/account/profile.md), [{{ui:account.security.securityTitle}}](../../docs/user/guide/account/account-security.md) and [{{ui:mfa.pageTitle}}](../../docs/user/guide/account/mfa.md). The same section covers [signing in](../../docs/user/guide/account/sign-in.md), [choosing a workspace](../../docs/user/guide/account/workspaces.md) and your [{{ui:notifications.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/account/notifications.md).

Each page guide explains what the page is for, who can use it, what every part of it shows, what each button does, and what to do when something goes wrong.

The **{{ui:admin.aiFeatures.assistant.name}}** in the corner of every workspace page answers questions about your workspace's compliance data. It can be turned on or off on the [{{ui:admin.aiFeatures.heading}}](../../docs/user/guide/admin/ai-features.md) page. For a longer question, see [{{ui:agents.ask.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/comply/ask.md).

## Get something done

The how-to guides walk through one task from start to finish.

- **Set up your workspace:** [invite a colleague](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/invite-a-colleague.md), [change a member's role](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/change-a-members-role.md), [create an API key](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/create-an-api-key.md), [connect an agent you run](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/connect-an-agent-you-run.md), [connect a scanner](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/connect-a-scanner.md), [set up single sign-on](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/set-up-single-sign-on.md) and [turn on multi-factor authentication](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/turn-on-multi-factor-authentication.md).
- **Get started:** [add a framework](../../docs/user/how-to/get-started/add-a-framework.md) and [attach evidence to a control](../../docs/user/how-to/get-started/attach-evidence-to-a-control.md).
- **Policies:** [create a policy](../../docs/user/how-to/policies/create-a-policy.md), [publish it](../../docs/user/how-to/policies/publish-a-policy.md) and [collect acknowledgements](../../docs/user/how-to/policies/collect-policy-acknowledgements.md).
- **Prepare for an audit:** [set up an audit cycle](../../docs/user/how-to/audits/set-up-an-audit-cycle.md), [build an audit pack](../../docs/user/how-to/audits/build-an-audit-pack.md) and [share it with your auditor](../../docs/user/how-to/audits/share-an-audit-pack.md).
- **Review access:** [run an access review](../../docs/user/how-to/access-reviews/run-an-access-review.md) and [record its decisions](../../docs/user/how-to/access-reviews/record-access-review-decisions.md).

Use the search at the top of any page to find a page, a button or a message by name.
