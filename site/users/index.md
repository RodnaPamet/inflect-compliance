Inflect Compliance helps your organisation run its compliance programme in one place: the risks you face, the controls that address them, the evidence that proves they work, and the audits that check it.

## Find your way around

The guides follow the app's own sidebar. If you are looking at a page in the app, its guide sits under the same heading here.

- **{{ui:nav.govern}}** covers your assets, risks and controls. Start with the [{{ui:risks.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/govern/risks.md).

Each page guide explains what the page is for, who can use it, what every part of it shows, what each button does, and what to do when something goes wrong.

## Get something done

The how-to guides walk through one task from start to finish.

- **Set up your workspace:** [invite a colleague](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/invite-a-colleague.md), [change a member's role](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/change-a-members-role.md), [set up single sign-on](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/set-up-single-sign-on.md), [turn on multi-factor authentication](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/turn-on-multi-factor-authentication.md) and [create an API key](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/create-an-api-key.md).

Use the search at the top of any page to find a page, a button or a message by name.
