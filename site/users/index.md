Inflect Compliance helps your organisation run its compliance programme in one place: the risks you face, the controls that address them, the evidence that proves they work, and the audits that check it.

## Find your way around

The guides follow the app's own sidebar. If you are looking at a page in the app, its guide sits under the same heading here.

- **{{ui:nav.govern}}** covers your assets, risks and controls. Start with the [{{ui:risks.title}}](../../docs/user/guide/govern/risks.md).

Each page guide explains what the page is for, who can use it, what every part of it shows, what each button does, and what to do when something goes wrong.

## Get something done

The how-to guides walk through one task from start to finish.

- **Set up your workspace:** [invite a colleague](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/invite-a-colleague.md), [change a member's role](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/change-a-members-role.md), [create an API key](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/create-an-api-key.md), [set up single sign-on](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/set-up-single-sign-on.md) and [turn on multi-factor authentication](../../docs/user/how-to/set-up/turn-on-multi-factor-authentication.md).
- **Get started:** [add a framework](../../docs/user/how-to/get-started/add-a-framework.md) and [attach evidence to a control](../../docs/user/how-to/get-started/attach-evidence-to-a-control.md).
- **Policies:** [create a policy](../../docs/user/how-to/policies/create-a-policy.md), [publish it](../../docs/user/how-to/policies/publish-a-policy.md) and [collect acknowledgements](../../docs/user/how-to/policies/collect-policy-acknowledgements.md).
- **Prepare for an audit:** [set up an audit cycle](../../docs/user/how-to/audits/set-up-an-audit-cycle.md), [build an audit pack](../../docs/user/how-to/audits/build-an-audit-pack.md) and [share it with your auditor](../../docs/user/how-to/audits/share-an-audit-pack.md).
- **Review access:** [run an access review](../../docs/user/how-to/access-reviews/run-an-access-review.md) and [record its decisions](../../docs/user/how-to/access-reviews/record-access-review-decisions.md).

Use the search at the top of any page to find a page, a button or a message by name.
